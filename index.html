About Us

FROM AN IDEA TO AN EXPERIENCE.

What began in 2017 as a management company fielding vendor requests — stages, launches, banners, canopies, décor — became too dependent on outside vendors to guarantee quality. So by 2018 we built our own production house. Today, Jubilant Events & Productions works with clients from concept to completion, creating unique, memorable experiences with a dedicated, in-house team.
