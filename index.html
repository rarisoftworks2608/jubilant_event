About Us

FROM AN IDEA TO AN EXPERIENCE.

Dream Cast Events began in 2017 as a management company. As client requirements grew, we started handling stages, launches, banners, printing, canopies, furniture, décor and weddings. Dependence on external vendors created challenges with quality and timelines.

We decided to build our own production house. By 2018, Jubilant Events & Productions was born with a dedicated management team and production capabilities.

Today, we work with clients from concept to completion, creating unique and memorable experiences with a strong focus on quality, service and execution.
