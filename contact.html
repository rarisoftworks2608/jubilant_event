Common queries

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers regarding bookings, execution capabilities and engagement timelines.

How early should we initiate event planning?

For major summits, exhibitions or custom multi-level stage builds, we recommend engaging 4 to 8 weeks in advance for 3D modelling and structural planning. However, with our fully owned in-house inventory, we routinely accommodate urgent turnarounds.

Do you manage events outside Pune?

Yes. While our headquarters and primary fabrication facility are based in Pune, our fleet and technical crew regularly travel across Maharashtra, Goa and pan-India for corporate summits and destination celebrations.

Can we hire you purely for production & fabrication?

Absolutely. We work both ways: as a complete turnkey event management agency handling every detail, or directly as a technical production partner providing stage builds, aluminium trusses, canopies and lighting.

What is your typical turnaround time for a proposal?

Following an initial discovery discussion or enquiry form submission, our production directors typically deliver a comprehensive line-item scope and cost estimate within 24 to 48 hours.
