Our story

Built on experience, run in-house.

It started with the same questions, again and again: "Will you be able to do a small stage for us?" "Can you organize a launch event?" "Do you have canopies, pagoda, chairs?" As a management company founded in 2017, Dream Cast Events kept fielding these requests, and kept relying on outside vendors to deliver. We wanted quality, but with vendors in the middle, that quality was never fully in our hands.

The headaches of depending on other vendors pushed us to a decision: build our own production house. We spent the next year adding inventory, and by 2018 we were ready: a dedicated management team backed by our own production capability. Jubilant Group was born.

We make it our priority to understand our clients' objectives, working with them from concept to completion to create a unique, memorable event. We're a team of experienced event planners who pride ourselves on delivering first-class service to every client, big and small.
